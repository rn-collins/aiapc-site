Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026 · Governing law: Hawaii, United States

This Privacy Policy applies to AI is a Piece of Cake (AIAPC) at aiapc-site.vercel.app and Aloha AI Consulting. Both properties are operated by RN Collins. If you have questions, email collins.ra@husky.neu.edu.

1. Who We Are

AI is a Piece of Cake (AIAPC) is an AI literacy newsletter and product suite for professionals — lawyers, physicians, educators, and executives. Aloha AI Consulting is an independent AI advisory practice. Both are owned and operated by RN Collins, based in Hawaii, United States.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only what's necessary to provide our services:

  • Email address — when you subscribe to the newsletter, sign up for early access, submit a contact form, or purchase a product.
  • Name — optionally provided via the contact form or newsletter signup.
  • Message content — when you send us a message through the contact form.
  • Usage analytics — aggregated, anonymized data about how visitors use the site (pages visited, referral sources, general location at the country level). No personally identifiable data is captured in analytics.
  • Purchase information — if you buy a product on Gumroad, Gumroad processes the transaction. We receive your email address and name from Gumroad to fulfill your order. We do not receive or store payment card information.

We do not use cookies for tracking. We do not collect IP addresses, fingerprint browsers, or build behavioral profiles.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for specific, limited purposes:

  • To send the AIAPC newsletter and product announcements to subscribers who opted in.
  • To respond to contact form submissions and inquiries.
  • To deliver purchased products (guides, Skills Packs, Annual Bundle) to customers.
  • To send transactional emails related to your purchase or subscription (receipts, renewal reminders, product delivery).
  • To understand aggregate site usage so we can improve content and navigation.

We do not use your email address for advertising, retargeting, or any purpose beyond the above.

4. Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate the site and newsletter. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Beehiiv — powers the AIAPC newsletter. When you subscribe, your email (and optionally your name) is stored in Beehiiv's systems. Beehiiv Privacy Policy: beehiiv.com/privacy.
  • Formspree — handles contact form submissions. When you submit a contact form, your name, email, and message are processed by Formspree and forwarded to our inbox. Formspree Privacy Policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Vercel Analytics — provides anonymized, aggregated site analytics. Vercel does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information. Vercel Privacy Policy: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Gumroad — processes product purchases. Gumroad handles payment processing and sends us your email and name to fulfill your order. Gumroad Privacy Policy: gumroad.com/privacy.

We have no affiliation with these companies beyond using their services. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

5. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, license, or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes. Full stop.

6. Data Retention

We retain your email address and related information for as long as you are subscribed to the newsletter or have an active product relationship with us. If you unsubscribe or request deletion, we will remove your data from our active systems within 30 days. Note that third-party processors (Beehiiv, Formspree, Gumroad) may retain records per their own policies.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Unsubscribe — every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link. One click removes you from the list.
  • Access — you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — you can ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion — you can request that we delete your personal data from our records.

To exercise any of these rights, email collins.ra@husky.neu.edu with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 10 business days.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. Our site is served over HTTPS. Access to subscriber data is limited to the site operator (RN Collins). That said, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for professionals and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Hawaii, United States, without regard to conflict of law provisions. Any disputes arising under this policy will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Hawaii.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of the page. Material changes will be noted in the newsletter. Your continued use of our services after any change constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
