Common questions

Everything you need to know.

What exactly is in the Annual Bundle? ▼
The bundle includes every paid product AIAPC publishes in your 12-month window: your paid newsletter tier (12 months), all six mini-guides ($92 value), The Annual Address: State of AI for Professionals (January 2027), the Skills.md Pack, the AI Policy Implementation Kit, the AI Readiness Scorecard, and every Decision Card, Prompt Pack, workbook, and template suite we ship during your window. The product list above reflects everything planned — and anything additional we publish goes in automatically.
When do I get access? ▼
Immediately after purchase via Gumroad. Products that are already published download right away. Products that haven't launched yet — like the Annual Address in January 2027 — will be delivered to your email when they ship. No waiting, no separate purchases.
What if a product launches after I buy? ▼
You get it. Everything published within your 12-month window is included — automatically, at no extra charge. If we ship something new, bundle members receive it. That's the point of the bundle.
What happens at renewal? ▼
Your renewal is locked at $237 for year two — not whatever the bundle price is at that point. You're rewarded for being an early member. Renewal is optional; you'll receive a reminder before it comes up and can cancel at any time.
Is there a refund policy? ▼
Yes. If you're not satisfied for any reason within 30 days of purchase, email us at collins.ra@northeastern.edu and we'll issue a full refund, no questions asked. After 30 days, the purchase is non-refundable.
Can I buy individual products instead? ▼
Yes — every product in the bundle is also sold individually on Gumroad. The bundle exists for people who want the full catalog at a significant discount. If you only need one or two guides, individual purchases make sense. If you want everything, the bundle is the obvious choice.
