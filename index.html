What makes this different

The Case is written in the M&M conference tradition — the morbidity and mortality review model medicine uses to analyze failures without assigning blame. Not a callout. A mechanism. The Line presents genuine AI ethics edge cases without resolving them, the way a law school hypothetical does. The Ask answers specific professional questions publicly — the kind professionals are already asking in private. These sections are designed for risk-averse, credential-bearing professionals who learn from precedent and edge cases, not from optimism.

The Course Ladder

Three courses.
One layer at a time.

Course 1 explains what AI actually is — before you touch any tool. Without that foundation, every tool feels mysterious and every output feels random. The People

RN
RN Collins
Legal · Scientific · Strategic — Newsletter Author

Neuroscientist, JD Candidate at Northeastern Law, AI strategist, and fractional law clerk. RN writes the newsletter — all four guaranteed sections, every issue, in her voice. She's been using AI in regulated professional contexts since 2022, which means she's run into the failure modes that don't make it into the product demos.

Her background is in neuroscience and scientific research, which informs how she thinks about AI: systems that behave probabilistically, not deterministically, require the same rigor you'd apply to reading a study — attention to methodology, skepticism about edge cases, and clarity about what the data can and can't tell you.

She's also a journalist. Her published work spans cannabis law, psychedelic medicine policy, and biophilic design — all fields where the gap between what practitioners know and what the public understands is large, consequential, and rarely bridged well. AIAPC is the same project applied to AI.
